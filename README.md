**Privacy Policy for NODUS Core**

**(Last Updated: October 26, 2025)**

**1. Introduction**

Welcome to NODUS Core. Your privacy is critically important to us. Since Clarity Core is designed with privacy at its core, this Privacy Policy is straightforward.

NODUS Core is a mobile application that allows users to create, customize, and share quotes. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data. No registration or account creation is required to use our application.

**2. Information We Do NOT Collect**

NODUS Core is built with a privacy-first approach. We explicitly do **NOT**:

*   **Collect Personal Information:** We do not ask for, require, or collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
*   **Require Registration:** You can use all features of Clarity Core immediately without creating an account or providing any login credentials.
*   **Track Your Activity:** We do not track your usage of the app, the quotes you create, or your navigation within the app.
*   **Access Your Photos/Gallery (for Wallpaper):** The feature to change your device's wallpaper operates locally on your device. We do not access, upload, or store your personal photos or gallery content.
*   **Collect Device Identifiers:** We do not collect unique device identifiers (like Advertising ID, UDID, IMEI) for tracking or profiling.

**3. How the App Works (Data Flow)**

*   **Quote Creation & Customization:** All creation and customization of quotes (including text, colors, backgrounds, sizes, gradients, and filters) happens locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.
*   **Sharing to Social Networks:** When you choose to share a created quote to a social media platform (like Instagram, Twitter, Facebook, etc.), the standard sharing mechanism of your operating system is used. The image is passed directly from Clarity Core to your chosen social media app. We have no control over, nor do we receive any data from, what you share or how the social media platform handles that data. Please review the privacy policy of the social media platform you are using.
*   **Wallpaper Function:** When you use the feature to set a created quote as your device wallpaper, the operation is performed locally. We do not access your existing wallpapers or gallery.

**4. Age Restrictions**
NODUS Core is intended for users who are **13 years of age or older**. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, the risk of inadvertently collecting data from a minor is eliminated.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 is using the app, please contact us. As we do not collect data, we cannot identify users, but we can provide information about our data-less design.

**5. Third-Party Services**

*   **Social Media Platforms:** Sharing features will link to services outside of Clarity Core. These third-party sites have their own independent privacy policies. We have no responsibility or liability for their content or activities.
*   **App Stores:** Downloading the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store is subject to those platforms' terms and privacy policies.

**6. Data Security**

Since Clarity Core does not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is no risk of a data breach from our services related to your personal information. All your creative work remains securely on your personal device.

**7. Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

**8. Your Consent**

By using Clarity Core, you consent to our Privacy Policy, which is fundamentally a policy of non-collection and respect for your device's local storage.
